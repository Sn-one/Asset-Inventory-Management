How RFID Audit Works

  • Each asset with an RFID chip has a unique tag ID printed on the label
  • Stationary readers at each station automatically log scans when an asset passes through the gate
  • Mobile readers are used for periodic physical audits — scan assets and confirm their location
  • All scans are timestamped and recorded on the asset's detail page
  • Use this page to manually record mobile reader scans during inventory checks
